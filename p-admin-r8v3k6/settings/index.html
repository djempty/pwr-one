Tax reserve

Share of each realised net gain withheld. Applies to members who have not chosen a rate.
Shown as the segmented control in the member portal Settings.
Fixed annual date. Paid to the member’s verified bank.
Same two-person rule as redemptions and payouts.

Net gains and losses offset within each month; a net loss month releases reserve back, up to the year-to-date amount withheld. At year end the reserve is reconciled against each member’s realised net gain and an estimated rate. The reserve is held, not remitted to CRA.
