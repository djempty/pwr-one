An invitation expires after 72 hours. Sign-in is disabled until a passkey is registered and a backup authenticator app is set up.
Access policy
  • Network. Sign-in only from the corporate VPN or an allow-listed IP range (10.20.0.0/16, office egress). Anything else is refused before authentication.
  • Passkeys. Admins sign in with a passkey (Face ID or Touch ID). A backup authenticator app is set up per admin. No SMS.
  • Step-up. Face ID or Touch ID is asked again only for high-risk actions: bank account changes, club wire instruction changes, redemptions or early releases over $25,000, admin and role changes, the final month-end lock, and approving the cast of Board proxy votes. Routine approvals use a plain confirm.
  • Two-person approval on money. Deposits, redemptions, bank changes, standing instructions, statements and payouts need a preparer and a different approver. With two admins, the other one always approves.
  • Owner covers approvals. The Owner role may approve money as second approver, so an admin without the Administrator role (Mike Telford) can still approve what Eric Savics prepares. Multiple Owners are allowed; both admins are Owners. No one approves their own item.
  • Sessions. 15-minute idle sign-out. Every action is written to the audit log.
