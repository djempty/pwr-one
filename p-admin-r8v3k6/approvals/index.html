Timing. Deposits are usually confirmed by the next business day. Once approved they show to the member as Received, pending allocation until month-end processing (Oct 30), when the member’s share of club NAV is calculated; they take no part in returns until then. Redemptions are paid by the next business day after approval, at the lower of the last month-end value and the current value of the member’s share, from the same reserve rules as before.

Two-person rule. The person who prepares a money item cannot approve it; the other admin must. The Owner role may approve money as second approver. Routine approvals use a plain confirm and are recorded against the signed-in admin. High-risk ones (bank account changes, redemptions or early releases over $25,000) also need a Face ID or Touch ID step-up. Everything is written to the audit log. Names are visible to administrators; members only ever see member numbers.
