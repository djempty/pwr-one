Frequently asked questions

11 questions
How are deposits and redemptions processed, and is there a minimum?

The minimum contribution is $5,000.00, and no member may hold more than 20% of club NAV. Every deposit and redemption is submitted in the portal, confirmed with your authenticator code and reviewed by the administrators. Redemptions need administrator approval and are paid at the next month-end value, only to verified bank accounts in your own name. Deposits are usually confirmed by the next business day and show as Received, pending allocation until month-end processing (next on October 30), when your share of club NAV is calculated; a deposit does not take part in returns until then.

How do I deposit by wire?

Choose Deposit on Deposits & Redemptions, then Wire transfer. Enter the amount, confirm with your authenticator code and the wire instructions appear, including your unique reference (for example PL-0087-W), which must be in the memo. Send the wire from an account in your own name; third-party wires are returned. The minimum is $5,000.00 and the sender pays any bank wire fees. Funds are usually confirmed by the next business day once received and matched, and show as Received, pending allocation until month-end processing, when your share of club NAV is calculated. They do not take part in returns until then. Polarity will never email or change these instructions, so always confirm them in the portal.

Who decides what the club buys and sells?

Members set the investment mandate by annual vote. The elected Board directs execution within it. Within these limits, entry and exit timing is set by the club’s designated trader using technical analysis. Trades are reported to members after the fact. The mandate changes only by member vote.

How does voting work?

Each member has one vote. The majority of votes cast decides, provided quorum is met. Members vote at least annually on the investment mandate and elect the Board. The rationale for each option is shown before you vote, and you may change your vote until the poll closes. Results show counts only.

Are there any fees?

No. There is no management or performance fee; only disclosed expenses are charged to the club. See the Fee & expense schedule in Documents.

Where do I find statements and tax documents?

In Documents. Statements are issued quarterly, once the quarter’s last month-end value is locked. The annual financial statements and your year-end T5013 package are issued once the club’s year-end accounts are complete; your 2026 package is expected in February 2027.

How is tax handled?

The club does not set money aside for tax. Overview shows an estimate of tax on your club income so far this year; you set that aside yourself. It is an estimate only. Final figures are on your year-end T5013 package, and treatment is confirmed by the club’s accountant. Please speak with a tax adviser about your own position.

How do I add a bank account?

In Settings, under Linked bank accounts. Adding an account requires an authenticator code, and the account must be in your own name. A new account shows as verification pending and cannot receive redemptions until it is verified.

What happens if I lose my authenticator device?

Use one of your recovery codes, which you can view in Settings. If you have none, call the administrator. Portal access is suspended and your identity confirmed before access is restored. An authenticator code is required for every deposit, redemption and bank change.

How do I make a complaint, or ask about my privacy?

Send a message below with the topic “Complaint”. Complaints go to the same form and are logged and answered in writing. Your personal information is handled under the Privacy notice in Documents, in line with British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act.

Who do I contact urgently?

Call the administrator on +1 555 010 0148, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM ET. If you suspect unauthorised access to your account, call immediately so that access can be suspended.

Send a secure message
